# The Execution of [[Evan Yushong]] and [[Rumia Valzvein]]

## Overview

Ten years ago, the RIFT General Affair Division carried out a public execution that would send shockwaves through the political, corporate, and religious landscape of both [[Earth-1]] and the Lunar territories. The condemned were two figures of considerable standing: **[[Evan Yushong]]**, CEO of [[Deeper Blue Corporation]] and former General of [[LURM]], and **[[Rumia Valzvein]]**, a Cardinal of [[Church of Azotim]] and a member of the noble House of Valzvein — though that last affiliation would be erased within hours of her death.

The charge was the creation of **[[Children of the Void]]** — offspring born through a modified [[Vita-Serum]] protocol allegedly engineered by the cult of **[[Black Sun Cult|Black Sun]]**.

---

## The Allegations

At the peak of [[Black Sun Cult|Black Sun]]'s influence a decade ago, independent journalists and NGOs began uncovering a pattern. Documents, testimonies, and biological evidence suggested that [[Evan Yushong]] had been injected with a modified [[Vita-Serum]] — one that did not suppress [[The Void|the void]] within him but rather *harmonized* with it in a manner that allowed his biological material to carry [[The Void|the void]]'s imprint.

Over a period estimated at two years, Evan was alleged to have repeatedly engaged in intercourse with [[Rumia Valzvein]]. Each union resulted in a pregnancy lasting exactly one month. At the end of that month, Rumia would give birth to what investigators called **[[Children of the Void]]** — infants born with [[The Rift|Rift]]'s energy already integrated into their biology. The number of children produced remains disputed. Some sources claim dozens. Others claim the number was closer to a hundred.

These children, if the allegations were true, were not merely test subjects. They were living weapons. Vessels designed to carry [[The Void|the void]]'s will into the world without the need for serum injection, without the risk of overexceeder transformation, and without the oversight of any regulatory body.

---

## The Investigation

The RIFT General Affair Division ([[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]]) received the compiled reports from multiple independent sources. For three months, they conducted their own investigation, leveraging their authority across both [[Earth-1]] and Lunar jurisdictions. Witnesses were interviewed. Biological samples were analyzed. Financial records were traced.

The investigation concluded that the allegations were **credible and confirmed**.

No trial was held. Under the emergency protocols established after [[The Rift|Rift]]'s expansion, the [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]] held the authority to summarily execute individuals deemed a threat to "the stability of human civilization within [[The Rift|Rift]]'s domain." [[Evan Yushong]] and [[Rumia Valzvein]] were deemed such a threat.

---

## The Execution

The execution was held publicly at the **Central Plaza of Lunar Administrative Sector 7**, broadcast across all networks. Both Evan and Rumia were brought to the platform in white prison garments. Witnesses reported that neither struggled. Neither wept.

As they were led to the center of the platform, Evan turned to Rumia. She met his gaze. Without a word exchanged, they reached for each other's hands and held them tightly.

The [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]] officer read the charges aloud. When asked if they had any final words, Evan spoke first.

### [[Evan Yushong]]'s Last Words

> *"I do not expect forgiveness. I do not ask for understanding. What we did, we did because the world we were given was already broken. The Void is not our enemy — it is the only thing that will survive what is coming. Those children were not mistakes. They were the only future humanity had left. You will learn this. When you do, I hope you remember that I said it before they killed me for it."*

The crowd was silent. Then Rumia spoke. Her voice was calm — some later described it as peaceful.

### [[Rumia Valzvein]]'s Last Words

> *"I loved him. I love them. Every single one. Do not let them be forgotten. Do not let them be feared. They are not monsters. They are the bridge between what we were and what we must become. The Church taught me that Azotim's breath gives life to all things. Even [[The Void|the Void]]. Even us."*

She turned to Evan. She smiled.

> *"I am ready."*

They faced the executioners. Their hands never separated.

The sentence was carried out simultaneously — two rounds, two heads, one moment. Their bodies fell together. Their hands remained clasped.

## Aftermath and Reactions

### [[Fortj Kaelion]] — CEO of Kaelion Mega Corporation (then)

> *"Evan was one of my most capable executives. Deeper Blue was a cornerstone of our research division. I will not pretend to be anything less than disgusted by the allegations — and by the confirmation that they were true. The creation of life as a weapon is an abomination. But I also cannot pretend that the loss of that intellect, that experience, is anything less than a tragedy for human progress. The [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]] made their decision. I respect their authority. I do not have to agree with their methods. Two valuable souls were extinguished today. That is a loss none of us can afford."*

### Evanesa Titania — Pope of [[Church of Azotim]]

> *"[[Rumia Valzvein]] was a devoted Cardinal of our faith. She served the Church with unwavering dedication for seventeen years. She was kind. She was thoughtful. She believed, as I do, that Azotim's light reaches even into the darkest corners of existence. I do not know the full truth of what transpired between her and [[Evan Yushong]]. But I know that the [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]] conducted their investigation in secrecy, without Church oversight, without allowing Rumia the counsel of her own faith. I demand that this decision be reviewed. I demand that the Church's voice be heard. And I mourn for Rumia — not as a criminal, but as a lost daughter of Azotim."*

### [[House of Yushong]] (Official Statement)

> *"[[Evan Yushong]] was the heir of our house. He was a General of [[LURM]]. He was a CEO. He was a son, a brother, and a father to no child that we acknowledge. The [[House of Yushong]] demands a full apology from the RIFT General Affair Division. We demand that Evan's name be cleared. We demand that the records reflect that our house did not condone, support, or participate in any of the alleged activities. Evan acted alone — if he acted at all. We will not rest until his name is restored."*

### [[LURM]] (Official Statement)

> *"General [[Evan Yushong]] served [[LURM]] with distinction for over a decade. His tactical contributions to [[The Rift|Rift]] Exploration Corps remain part of our operational doctrine. We regret deeply that his path led him to this outcome. However, [[LURM]] respects the authority of the RIFT General Affair Division and their enforcement of regulations designed to protect human civilization. We will not interfere with their judgment. We mourn the loss of a capable officer. We condemn the actions that led to this moment."*

### House of Valzvein (Official Statement — Single Sentence)

> *"The House of Valzvein has no record of any individual named [[Rumia Valzvein]]."*

No further comment was ever issued. Within hours of the execution, Rumia's name was removed from all Valzvein family records. Her portraits were taken down. Her history was erased.

### RIFT General Affair Division (Official Statement — Issued at Execution)

> *"The sentence has been carried out. The threat has been neutralized. The RIFT General Affair Division does not issue apologies. We do not provide justifications beyond what has already been presented. Our mandate is the preservation of human civilization within [[The Rift|Rift]]'s domain. We have fulfilled that mandate. This matter is closed."*

To this day, the [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]] has never apologized, never revisited the case, and never released the full investigation records.

## The [[Children of the Void]]: What Happened to Them?

Officially: all [[Children of the Void]] were terminated or placed under permanent observation by the [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]].

Unofficially: rumors persist that some survived. That they were taken by [[Black Sun Cult|Black Sun]] remnants, or by sympathetic parties within the Church, or by individuals within [[LURM]] who saw tactical value in preserving the samples. Some say the children are kept in cryogenic storage in undisclosed locations. Others say they were scattered across [[Earth-2]] and the deeper Rift territories, raised in secret by families who never knew their true origin.

The truth is unknown. The [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]]'s records remain classified.

---

## Legacy

The execution of [[Evan Yushong]] and [[Rumia Valzvein]] became a symbol of the [[RIFT General Affair Division|RGAD]]'s absolute authority and the cost of operating outside its boundaries. It deepened [[The Rift|the rift]] between the noble houses and the regulatory bodies. It emboldened the Church's opposition to RGAD overreach. And it left a lingering question that no one dares to answer publicly:

*If the [[Children of the Void]] were the future, as Evan claimed — what does that say about the world that executed them?*
